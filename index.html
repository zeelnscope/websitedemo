Manufactured in India

Precision-cast in Morbi, finished for kitchens anywhere.

Stark Quartz Sink LLP casts every basin from a 10mm quartz composite body — dense enough to resist scratching, staining and everyday kitchen splashes, and finished by hand in granite or metallic tones so no two runs look factory-flat.

What ships in the box: coupling, strainer and connection pipes, so the sink is ready for top-mount, flush-mount or under-mount install without a second hardware run.

Why Stark

A quartz body built to outlast the kitchen around it.

The same core resistance sits in every finish — this is what the 10mm composite is for.

No Acid Proof

Resists everyday food, oil and turmeric splashes — clean with mild soap and water (avoid harsh acid cleaners).

Scratch resistant

Dense quartz composite shrugs off knives, pans and steel wool without dulling.

Heat resistant

Set a hot pan straight from the stove into the bowl without marking the finish.

No hard laundry chemicals needed

Cleans up with soap and water — no bleach or abrasive chemical cleaners required.

Non-porous, hygienic surface

A sealed quartz surface that doesn't harbour bacteria or absorb odours.

Quality assured

Every basin is checked for finish consistency and drain fit before it leaves Morbi.

Impact resistant

Absorbs the everyday drop of a pan or a plate better than stainless steel.

10mm uniform thickness

Cast at a consistent 10mm across every model, from compact to ultra-wide.
